# page.synthetic.png

A simulated page: generated from the zone polygons and the text of the page data. Nothing in it comes from a photograph of the register (the parchment is generated noise).

- Text is set in **UnifrakturMaguntia** (blackletter, textualis style), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Google Fonts). Only rendered pixels are distributed, not the font.
- Watermark font: DejaVu Sans Bold (Bitstream Vera licence).
- The diagonal watermark "SIMULATED PAGE" marks the image as a simulation; it is not a reading of the manuscript.
